# Architecture Update v1.3 — Change Log

Date: 2026-11-19

## Confirmed CEO decisions incorporated
- Long-term product direction remains BOTH: AI business and MMORPG/community.
- Near-term development priority is BUSINESS FIRST.
- Upgrade approval mode is AI RECOMMENDS, CEO APPROVES.

## Changes
- Updated Master Architecture to distinguish long-term product pillars from near-term business-first resource priority.
- Added business-first funding governance: development spend must be supported by available, confirmed and reconciled company funds after applicable costs, obligations, and CEO-defined reserves.
- Added milestone-based reinvestment, explicit CEO approval, cost caps, acceptance criteria, rollback plans, and re-approval for material overruns.
- Clarified that the AI Director may analyze and recommend but cannot approve budgets, move funds, purchase services, or authorize funded batches.
- Updated Implementation Roadmap with a required business-first reinvestment gate before major game-development spending.
- Added dependencies and CEO-approved funding gates to multiplayer, seamless-world, Adventure, and expansion phases.
- Kept player reward budgets separate from development funds and retained the previously approved hybrid game-reward rules.
- Preserved the original 100-item Layout Upgrade Roadmap unchanged.

## Preserved constraints
- Phase D v8 remains the protected immutable rollback baseline.
- No runtime code, user data, credentials, settings, orders, audit logs, or backups were modified.
- No feature is newly marked DONE; all new governance and funding controls remain documentation/planned until implemented and tested.
- No fixed revenue threshold or reinvestment percentage was invented. The CEO configures these using actual financial data.
